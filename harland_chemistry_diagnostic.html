This short check helps us understand what you already know so we can start you in the right place. It is not a test to pass or fail. Answer each question as best you can, and the check will adjust to you as you go.

How it works. You will see one section at a time. Answer every question in a section, then continue. The questions adjust to your answers, so you only see what is right for you.

There is no time limit. Most students finish the questions in about half an hour. At the end there is a short written section where you explain your thinking in your own words.

Before you begin. This diagnostic works best when it sees the real you. Please work on your own, without AI tools, notes, or help from others. There are no grades here and nothing to lose: an answer you guess or get help with can place you in lessons that don't fit, and that costs you time in the classes that follow. Do your best, be honest with yourself, and let the test do its job.
